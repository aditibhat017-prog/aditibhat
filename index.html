Aditi Bhat

Aditi is a Biomolecular Engineering and Bioinformatics graduate working at the intersection of biomedical devices, molecular biology, and computational health. She enjoys combining technical research, project management, and interdisciplinary leadership to develop solutions to real-world health challenges.

Her work has included founding early-stage biomedical projects, developing wearable sensing concepts, and leading UCSC's 2025 iGEM team to a Silver Medal at the iGEM Grand Jamboree. Outside of research, Aditi trains for triathlons, writes the Hindu mythology-inspired Devas Academy Chronicles, and creates wire-art and 3D-printed jewelry.
