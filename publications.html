Awards & Honors

iGEM Venture Foundry — 2nd Place · 2026

GaitMate placed second for the grand prize in the Americas regional Venture Creation Lab program, competing among 40 early-stage teams developing biotechnology and bioengineering ventures.

Baskin Engineering Dean's Award · 2026

Award recognizing exceptional achievements by undergraduate students who completed a research project or creative inquiry activity during the academic year. Aditi received the award in association with her leadership and research contributions to UCSC's 2025 iGEM project.

Summa Cum Laude · 2025

University honors at UC Santa Cruz are based on a review of graduates' academic records, with honors generally limited to 15 percent of the graduating class. University Honors are based on cumulative UC GPA, while major honors may incorporate additional faculty review criteria.

iGEM Silver Medal · 2025

Awarded to iGEM teams that successfully address key pillars of an iGEM project, including Engineering Success and Human Practices. UCSC's SafeTEA team received a Silver Medal at the 2025 iGEM Grand Jamboree in Paris.

Cynthia Mathews Research Scholarship · 2025

Scholarship awarded to UC Santa Cruz students pursuing studies or internships related to reproductive rights, health policy, healthcare professions, or public health. Established in 2007, the scholarship honors Cynthia Mathews' 40 years of service in reproductive health.
