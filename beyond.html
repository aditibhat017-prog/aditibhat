Triathlon

Swimming, cycling, running, and endurance training.

Aditi has been swimming competitively for more than ten years. After graduating from college, she began transitioning into triathlon training as a new way to challenge herself, build confidence outside of academics and work, and combine her love of swimming with running and cycling. She is currently training toward IRONMAN 70.3 Santa Cruz in September 2027, with the long-term goal of completing a full-distance IRONMAN.

Writing

Fiction, mythology, poetry, and personal essays.

Aditi has always enjoyed writing fiction and poetry. She is currently developing The Mark of Shiva, the first novel in her fantasy series, The Devas Academy Chronicles. Inspired by Hindu mythology and her own experiences growing up with Hindu culture, the series combines mythology, fantasy, and contemporary storytelling. The Mark of Shiva is planned for publication on November 8, 2026.

Aditi also writes essays on Medium about leadership, engineering, women in STEM, and her experiences as a first-generation engineering student and graduate.

Jewelry & 3D Design

Wire art, 3D modeling, printing, and handmade design.

Aditi has been creating simple wire-art jewelry for friends and family for years. Her early introduction to tools such as Tinkercad also sparked an interest in 3D modeling, design, and fabrication. More recently, she has begun combining these interests to experiment with custom 3D-printed and wire-art jewelry, bringing digital design and hands-on making into the same creative process.
